On the Stellenbosch University Developer Society

2026-08-07

In the second semester of 2025, Stephen Carter, Veer Gosai, and I co-founded the Stellenbosch University Developer Society (SUDS). This piece is about what I intend the society to be.

The three foundational goals of the society (chosen by Stephen, Veer, and I) were to:

  • help students build projects and explore technology,
  • help students build friendships and relationships with peers, and
  • foster relationships between students and the South African technology and software industries.

Insofar as there are members of the society that make these things happen, they are instantiating the society.

2025 was not the first time I tried to start the society. The biggest lesson I took away from trying two previous times is that it is really important to spread the thing that keeps the society alive outside of the founder. You want the society to be robust to loss of the founder's motivation, or some other threat that will prevent it from persisting. In SUDS's case, it needed humans who believed in its goals, who were willing to work to implement them, and who are able to find other humans who will maintain it in the future.

"Something exists to the degree that it's implemented." - Joscha Bach
If there are not individuals willing to take intiative and organise events, the society will not be implemented and it will cease to exist.

If, like me, you find yourself looking for nerds to hangout with, then you know what to do. You don't have to ask permission, you don't have to fall in with anything that exists or came before you. You can just build the thing that you wish existed. It just takes work.
