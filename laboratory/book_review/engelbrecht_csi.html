Introduction

I think this applies to 'the great man theory of history' debate:

There exists a tight coupling between individual and collective behavior: the collective behavior of individuals shapes and dictates the behavior of the swarm. On the other hand, swarm behavior has an influence on the conditions under which each individual performs its actions. These actions may change the environment, and thus the behaviors of that individual and its neighbors may also change – which again may change the collective swarm behavior.
