Machine Learning

One lesson I learnt this month is that there are different levels of understanding. Algorithms for machine learning models can be complicated and may have many different components. Just because you understand all the individual components that make up an algorhtm and you're able to implement it, does not mean you understand the general behaviour of the algorithm. Studying all the parts of an algorithm can nurture an intuitive understanding of the whole, but it is not the only component. I felt this way after studying Shannon's measure for entropy as used in decision trees, and after I understood it it felt like I was no closer to really knowing how decision trees work.
